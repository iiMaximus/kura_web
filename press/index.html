Short description

Lychee is a food and cosmetic ingredient scanner for iOS and Android. Scan a barcode or ingredient label to understand processing, additives, allergens, nutrition, and cosmetic ingredient concerns. A processing-first food scanner with personalized allergen checks and a separate beta cosmetic scoring model. Free to download with core barcode scanning and product scores. Optional Lychee Premium unlocks additional analysis and features. The free experience may include ads; Premium includes ad-free scanning.

About Lychee

Lychee helps people understand everyday product labels: food processing, additives, allergens, nutrition and cosmetic ingredients. It is independently developed by Maksym Horoszczak for iOS and Android.

Scoring methodology

Lychee uses a processing-first food model and a separate beta cosmetic ingredient model. Both depend on the information available; a missing or uncertain label should not produce fake confidence.
