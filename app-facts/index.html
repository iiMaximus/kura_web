Features

What Lychee can help you do.

Food barcode scanning

Look up packaged foods through Open Food Facts and review available ingredients, allergens, nutrition, NOVA and Nutri-Score data.

Processing-first food score

A 0–100 comparison score that prioritizes processing and also considers additives, ingredient quality and nutrition. Incomplete data can limit or prevent a score.

Personalized allergen checks

Compare available product ingredients, allergens and trace statements with a selected profile. Results do not guarantee the absence of an allergen.

Ingredient-label scanning

Read a food or cosmetic ingredient paragraph with OCR when barcode data is missing or incomplete. Review the extracted text against the package.

Cosmetic barcode scanning

Look up cosmetics and personal-care products through Open Beauty Facts and review INCI ingredients, functions and concerns.

Beta cosmetic score

A separate ingredient concern model that considers ingredient coverage, product type and exposure. Low coverage can show Not enough data instead of a score.

Pantry and scan history

Save products and return to previous scans to compare everyday purchases.

Optional Premium

Additional AI explanations, ingredient insights, healthier swaps and ad-free scanning. Availability and offers may differ by platform or app version.

Limits

What a scan does not establish.

  • Lychee provides educational label information, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or a guarantee of product safety.
  • Database records, OCR text and AI explanations can contain errors. Check the current packaging and manufacturer information.
  • An ingredient concern is not a measurement of concentration or individual risk. Cosmetic exposure and personal sensitivity matter.
  • A barcode or ingredient photo cannot measure heavy metals or microplastics. Estimates and separately sourced laboratory results must be distinguished.
  • Coverage varies by product, region and available data. Lychee does not guarantee recognition of every barcode or ingredient.
  • App features, Premium access and offers can change. Check the current app-store listing and in-app offer.
