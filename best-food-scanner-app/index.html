What matters to you?

Four questions to ask before choosing.

Processing

Does the app give processing a central role, or mainly summarize nutrient balance? Lychee takes a processing-first approach.

Personal requirements

Can you inspect ingredients and trace statements against your own profile? Does the app clearly explain what it cannot guarantee?

Incomplete products

Can it show missing data and let you read or contribute a label, rather than inventing a confident answer?

Also check pricing, ads, account requirements and the difference between free and paid features in the current store listing.

When Lychee fits

Processing, ingredient checks and beauty in one app.

Consider Lychee when you want a processing-first food score, personalized ingredient and allergen checks, an ingredient-label input when barcode data is incomplete, and a separate beta cosmetic model. Try several products you buy regularly to assess coverage in your area.

If you are comparing it with Yuka, read the methodology comparison and check both apps directly. This guide is published by Lychee; it is not an independent ranking of all scanners.

Common questions

Before you scan.

Is Lychee free?

Free to download with core barcode scanning and product scores. Optional Lychee Premium unlocks additional analysis and features. The free experience may include ads; Premium includes ad-free scanning. See the current in-app offer or app-store listing for local prices, trial eligibility, and subscription terms.

How does Lychee score food?

Lychee uses a 0–100 food score that prioritizes processing. Additives, ingredient quality, sugar, salt, saturated fat, fiber and protein also matter. Available NOVA data and ingredient-based estimates inform processing; Lychee applies its own scoring rules. Missing data can reduce confidence or prevent a score.

Can Lychee check food allergens?

Lychee helps compare available ingredient, allergen and trace information with a selected profile. It cannot guarantee that a food is allergen-free or detect undeclared cross-contact. Always read the current label and follow your allergy care plan.
