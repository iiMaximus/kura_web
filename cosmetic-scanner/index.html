What you can scan

One app for the bathroom shelf too.

Skincare and makeup

Inspect cleansers, moisturizers, serums, foundation, mascara and lip products for ingredient functions and concern categories.

Hair and body care

Read shampoo, conditioner, styling products, deodorant, soap, body wash and lotion labels.

Use context

Rinse-off products, leave-on formulas, fragrances, oral products, eye products and lip products have different exposure assumptions.

Beta, with visible limits

A score should earn your confidence.

Lychee checks ingredient recognition and product context before showing a cosmetic score. Insufficient coverage shows Not enough data. You can still inspect recognized ingredients and unknown names.

The score considers ingredient concern, coverage and exposure. It does not know every ingredient concentration, your complete skin history or the behavior of the finished formulation. Read the cosmetic methodology for the coverage rules and exceptions.

Incomplete records

A missing ingredient list is not a missing product.

A cosmetic product may exist in Open Beauty Facts but have no ingredients attached. Lychee can show the available product information and invite an ingredient contribution. Check the package and use label scanning where useful.

For sensitivity questions

Lychee can help identify label terms to discuss with a dermatologist or allergist. It cannot diagnose contact allergy, acne triggers or pregnancy safety, or verify sunscreen performance.

Common questions

Before you scan.

Is Lychee free?

Free to download with core barcode scanning and product scores. Optional Lychee Premium unlocks additional analysis and features. The free experience may include ads; Premium includes ad-free scanning. See the current in-app offer or app-store listing for local prices, trial eligibility, and subscription terms.

Does Lychee scan cosmetics and skincare?

Yes. Lychee scans beauty and personal-care barcodes and ingredient labels. Its beta cosmetic score considers recognized ingredients, concerns and exposure, including rinse-off versus leave-on use. It shows Not enough data when ingredient coverage is insufficient.

What if my product is missing from the database?

Try reading the ingredient label with Lychee. You can also submit or correct product data through its contribution flow. OCR and contributed records need checking; a missing ingredient list should be treated as incomplete data.
