About Lychee

For the moment you turn a package around.

Lychee helps people understand everyday product labels: food processing, additives, allergens, nutrition and cosmetic ingredients. It is independently developed by Maksym Horoszczak for iOS and Android.

Why Lychee exists

A label should help you make a choice.

Ingredient paragraphs and front-of-pack claims can pull in different directions. Lychee brings the available product data into a readable breakdown so you can inspect what matters before buying, eating or applying a product.

Its food philosophy prioritizes processing without ignoring nutrition. Its cosmetic philosophy is cautious and exposure-aware: recognized ingredients, unknown names and data limits should be visible.

Who builds it

An independent app, with a public identity.

Lychee is developed by Maksym Horoszczak, the developer named in its official app-store listing. Lychee is the current brand; older references may say Kura or Allergify. Native package identifiers keep the older name for store continuity.

Lychee is free to download, with core scanning and optional Premium features. The free experience may include advertising. Advertising and product scoring are different parts of the app; no paid placement is presented here as a scoring criterion.

Lychee is a food and cosmetic ingredient scanner for iOS and Android. Scan a barcode or ingredient label to understand processing, additives, allergens, nutrition, and cosmetic ingredient concerns.

Scoring methodology

Lychee uses a processing-first food model and a separate beta cosmetic ingredient model. Both depend on the information available; a missing or uncertain label should not produce fake confidence.

Read more →

Data sources

Lychee’s barcode lookups use Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts. Label photos use OCR; scores use Lychee’s own logic. Public scientific and regulatory references provide context, not a live laboratory test.
