More than a chemical name

Function, context and the rest of the label.

What it does

An additive may provide color, preserve a product, maintain texture or sweeten it. Its function helps explain the formulation.

Why it is flagged

A concern category or regulatory reference deserves explanation. It does not establish individual risk at an unknown concentration.

How it affects the score

Lychee considers recognized additives alongside processing, ingredient quality and nutrition, rather than counting additives alone.

Regulatory context

Approval depends on the ingredient and use.

US and EU additive rules can differ. Approval, restrictions and conditions of use should be checked against current official sources for the exact ingredient, product category and jurisdiction. A blanket “banned in Europe” claim can erase these details.

Common questions

Before you scan.

How does Lychee score food?

Lychee uses a 0–100 food score that prioritizes processing. Additives, ingredient quality, sugar, salt, saturated fat, fiber and protein also matter. NOVA and Nutri-Score are inputs, not the entire score. Missing data can reduce confidence or prevent a score.

What if my product is missing from the database?

Try reading the ingredient label with Lychee. You can also submit or correct product data through its contribution flow. OCR and contributed records need checking; a missing ingredient list should be treated as incomplete data.
