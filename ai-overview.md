## Features

- **Food barcode scanning:** Look up packaged foods through Open Food Facts and review available ingredients, allergens, nutrition and processing information.
- **Processing-first food score:** A 1–100 comparison score that prioritizes processing and also considers additives, ingredient quality and nutrition. Incomplete data can limit or prevent a score.
- **Personalized allergen checks:** Compare available product ingredients, allergens and trace statements with a selected profile. Results do not guarantee the absence of an allergen.
- **Ingredient-label scanning:** Read a food or cosmetic ingredient paragraph with OCR when barcode data is missing or incomplete. Review the extracted text against the package.
- **Cosmetic barcode scanning:** Look up cosmetics and personal-care products through Open Beauty Facts and review INCI ingredients, functions and concerns.
- **Beta cosmetic score:** A separate ingredient concern model that considers ingredient coverage, product type and exposure. Low coverage can show Not enough data instead of a score.
- **Pantry and scan history:** Save products and return to previous scans to compare everyday purchases.
- **Optional Premium:** Additional AI explanations, ingredient insights, healthier swaps and ad-free scanning. Availability and offers may differ by platform or app version.

## Direct product data

- [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/): Food barcode and product-label data. Collaborative records can be incomplete, outdated or different from the package in your hand.
- [Open Beauty Facts](https://world.openbeautyfacts.org/): Cosmetic barcode and product-label data. Product coverage and ingredient completeness vary.

## Limits

- Lychee provides educational label information, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or a guarantee of product safety.
- Database records, OCR text and AI explanations can contain errors. Check the current packaging and manufacturer information.
- An ingredient concern is not a measurement of concentration or individual risk. Cosmetic exposure and personal sensitivity matter.
- A barcode or ingredient photo cannot measure heavy metals or microplastics. Estimates and separately sourced laboratory results must be distinguished.
- Coverage varies by product, region and available data. Lychee does not guarantee recognition of every barcode or ingredient.
- App features, Premium access and offers can change. Check the current app-store listing and in-app offer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Lychee?

Lychee is a food and cosmetic ingredient scanner for iOS and Android. Scan a barcode or ingredient label to understand processing, additives, allergens, nutrition, and cosmetic ingredient concerns. Its food score prioritizes processing; cosmetics use a separate beta model.

### Is Lychee free?

Free to download with core barcode scanning and product scores. Optional Lychee Premium unlocks additional analysis and features. The free experience may include ads; Premium includes ad-free scanning. See the current in-app offer or app-store listing for local prices, trial eligibility, and subscription terms.

### Is Lychee available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download Lychee from the Apple App Store or Google Play using the official links on this website. Store availability and device compatibility vary by region.

### How does Lychee score food?

Lychee uses a 1–100 food score that prioritizes processing. Additives, ingredient quality, sugar, salt, saturated fat, fiber and protein also matter. Available NOVA data and ingredient-based estimates inform processing; Lychee applies its own scoring rules. Missing data can reduce confidence or prevent a score.

### Does Lychee scan cosmetics and skincare?

Yes. Lychee scans beauty and personal-care barcodes and ingredient labels. Its beta cosmetic score considers recognized ingredients, concerns and exposure, including rinse-off versus leave-on use. It shows Not enough data when ingredient coverage is insufficient.

### Can Lychee check food allergens?

Lychee helps compare available ingredient, allergen and trace information with a selected profile. It cannot guarantee that a food is allergen-free or detect undeclared cross-contact. Always read the current label and follow your allergy care plan.

### What if my product is missing from the database?

Try reading the ingredient label with Lychee. You can also submit or correct product data through its contribution flow. OCR and contributed records need checking; a missing ingredient list should be treated as incomplete data.

### Where does Lychee get product data?

Food barcode lookup uses Open Food Facts; cosmetic barcode lookup uses Open Beauty Facts. Label photos use OCR. Lychee applies its own scoring logic and ingredient dictionaries to available information.

### Does Lychee use AI to calculate every score?

The food and cosmetic scores use scoring logic and ingredient dictionaries. Optional AI assistance adds explanations and analysis; it is not a laboratory test or a guarantee that a label is accurate.

### Can Lychee detect heavy metals or microplastics?

A barcode or label photo cannot measure contaminants. Where Lychee presents estimates or published lab results, those are different forms of evidence. Check the source, tested product, date and limits before drawing a conclusion.

### Does Lychee work offline?

Previously saved product information can be available from local history or pantry data. New barcode lookups need network access; failed scans can be queued for retry. Offline access is not a complete offline product database.

Lychee is independently developed by Maksym Horoszczak. Its current public name is Lychee; older references may say Kura or Allergify. See the official store listings for the developer and current release details.
